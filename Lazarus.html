The Story Of Lazarus The BestFriend Of Jesus

Few fact about Jesus Bestfriend Lazarus


Lazarus lived in the village of Bethany with his two sisters, Mary and Martha. Their family was very close to Jesus,He often visited their home, ate with them, and found comfort in their friendship.

One day,Lazarus became very sick, and his sisters sent a message to Jesus saying, “Lord, the one you love is sick.” This shows how close Jesus was to Lazarus. Instead of going immediately,Jesus waited two more days,telling His disciples that Lazarus sickness would bring glory to God. By the time Jesus arrived in Bethany, Lazarus had already been dead for four days. The whole town was mourning Martha ran to Jesus, saying,“Lord, if you had been here,my brother would not have died.” Jesus comforted her and declared,“I am the resurrection and the life.” When Mary came, Jesus saw her crying he was deeply moved and then the Bible says,“Jesus wept.” It shows His deep love for Lazarus and His compassion for human pain. Jesus went to the tomb and told the people to roll away the stone.Then He prayed and shouted,“Lazarus, come out!” To everyone shock, Lazarus walked out of the tomb, alive, still wrapped in burial cloths. This miracle amazed the crowd and led many people to believe in Jesus. It also became one of the final events leading to Jesus crucifixion, because His enemies felt threatened by His growing influence.

Note:Lazarus story shows Jesus power over death and his deep love for His friends. It is one of the greatest signs of Jesus identity as the Son of God.

Key Facts About Lazarus

  • Lazarus lived in Bethany,a village near Jerusalem.
  • Lazarus had two sisters, Mary and Martha.
  • The Bible says Jesus loved Lazarus and was very close to their family.
  • The story of Lazarus can be found in John 11:1-44 and mentioned again in chapter 12.

About Lazarus

  • Lazarus was dead for four days before Jesus raised him back to life.
  • Jesus wept when He saw the sorrow of Mary and Martha over Lazarus death.
  • Lazarus resurrection led many people to believe in Jesus.
